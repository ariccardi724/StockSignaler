How the signals work

Details — all six signals
#1 MA-band + RSIQQQ touches 290- or 330-day MA (day low within 1%), hold 10 days; 420-day, hold 20 days. RSI 25–30 = full size, 35–45 = skip.
#2 VIX 3-down-dayArm VIX ≥30, buy close of 3rd straight VIX down day while VIX ≥25, hold 20 days. Capitulation: VIX ≥40 peak in prior 10 days, then 2nd down day.
#3 Dip buyClose ≤ 5% below lowest close in prior 63 trading days, hold 5 days. Backtest (253 held tickers, past year): 65.8% win, n=260, +4.6% mean.
#4 RSI(2)2-day RSI under 10 at the close, exit when RSI(2) crosses over 50 or after 5 trading days. Literature: ~70–80% win on index ETFs; not yet backtested on this universe.
#5 Bollinger %BClose below the lower Bollinger Band (20-day, 2σ), then close back inside the band. Hold 5 days. Literature: low-60s% win at 5 days; not yet backtested on this universe.
#6 ORB 15-min15-min close above the 9:30–9:45am ET opening-range high, hold to the close. Literature: ~45–55% win, winners bigger than losers; intraday only.
ExitsClock exits only — no stop-loss. Stops made every backtest worse (signals #1–#3).

Recommended buys — today only

Built live from today's signals (#1–#5) and the latest EOD scan. Each one is a quick-enter order: price, shares and $ are editable and cross-computed. Log it and it moves to the Trade Log and the Calendar. Don't log it and it disappears at midnight ET.

Calendar — sells and possible buys

Sell events are placed automatically from logged buys, on that signal's clock exit. Possible buys are today's recommendations — editable, and they disappear at midnight ET if you don't log them. Click a day to expand it.

Trade log — log an entry, get your sell target

Sell target is a clock exit: signal #1 = 10 trading days, signal #2 = 20, signals #3 / #4 / #5 = 5, signal #6 = to the close the same day. Weekends and US market holidays (2026–2027) are skipped. The app shows the live price for open trades and the reference close once the sell date arrives — it never predicts a future exit price.

Backtests only — past win rates are not a future guarantee, and this app places no trades. Signals are computed from fetched closes in your browser.
